## Notes on evidence quality

**[judgement]**

1. **Two key papers are paywalled** (S2 Katz 2015, S40 Spalding 2017). Spalding's headline
   figures are consistently reported across independent secondary sources, so I treat them
   as reliable. Katz 2015 is the one to chase — it is the source of the hyperpycnal-flow
   mechanism the whole project rests on, and possibly of the global-coastline figure. **HTU
   library access would resolve both.**
2. **Most regional sources are `[abstract]`.** Sufficient for a market scan; **not**
   sufficient for a scientific claim. If any of these facts moves onto a slide as a
   quantitative claim, read the full paper first.
3. **Nothing here is a substitute for a patent search.** This scan covers published systems
   and scientific literature. The concept doc §5.2 also mentions patents; that remains
   undone.
