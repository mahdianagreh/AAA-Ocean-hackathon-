**Part C. Who pays for the first three years, and which policy instruments create the
obligation to buy.**

## 2 · The funding instruments that actually fit

### 2.1 GFCR "Gulf of Aqaba and Northern Red Sea Resilient Reefs Programme" — the target

Everything below is **[sourced]**, read directly from the signed GFCR Concept Note
([UNDP MPTF](https://mptf.undp.org/sites/default/files/documents/2022-12/gulf_of_aqaba_and_n_red_sea_reefs_programme_redacted.pdf),
DocuSigned 3 Nov / 5 Dec 2022).

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Programme | Gulf of Aqaba and Northern Red Sea Resilient Reefs Programme |
| Recipient / convening agent | **UNDP** |
| Countries | **Jordan and Egypt** (Gulf of Aqaba) |
| Priority sites | **Jordan: Aqaba Marine Reserve.** Egypt: Abu Galum MRPA, Nabq MRPA, Ras Mohammed National Park |
| Preparatory grant | **USD 199,865** |
| Full programme grant need | **USD 10 million** from the GFCR Grant Window, plus USAID Egypt co-financing |
| Expected leverage | **at least USD 30 million** additional private finance |
| Implementation window | **2023 → end of 2030**; phases: Inception (~1.5–2 yr), Consolidation (~3 yr), Growth (~2 yr) |
| Co-implementers | **IUCN Regional Office for West Asia** (Hany El Shaer, Regional Director) · **CORDAP** (Carlos M. Duarte, Executive Director) |
| Jordan focal point | **Randa Aboul-Hosn, UNDP Jordan Resident Representative** |

**Three quotes from the document that matter more than anything else in this scan:**

> Key threats to coral reefs include … **climate change (which may … change rainfall
> patterns / intensity that could increase flows of freshwater, sediments and pollution onto
> coral reefs).**

> **Outcome 1 — Protect:** … **Water quality/land-ocean interface projects roll-out to
> protect coral reefs**

> Aqaba Marine Park [objectives]: **Monitoring of marine water quality, sediment and
> pollution levels**, and fish/coral reef communities

**[judgement]** Read those together. The funding document for a $10M programme whose
priority site is Aqaba Marine Reserve:

1. names **increased sediment flows from changed rainfall intensity** as a key threat,
2. funds **land-ocean interface** work as a protection outcome, and
3. records that the Aqaba Marine Park's own objectives already include **sediment
   monitoring**.

ReefShield is not adjacent to this programme. **It is a missing component of it.** The pitch
does not need to argue that the problem matters — UNDP, IUCN and the Government of Jordan
have already signed a document saying it does. The pitch needs to say *we built the
land-ocean interface tool you funded an outcome for*.

**Note on Saudi Arabia [sourced]:** Saudi Arabia is **not a GFCR recipient country** and no
Saudi partner receives GFCR grant funding, but it is a **strategic partner**, with **UNDP
Saudi Arabia as the conduit**. So the Saudi commercial route (NEOM, Red Sea Global) stays
commercial — grants will not reach it.

### 2.2 Related instruments in the same programme family

**[sourced]**

- **USAID Egypt's Red Sea Coral Reef Initiative (RSCRI)** — establishing a Conservation Trust
  Fund plus a **US$15 million Blended Finance Facility**, with USAID envisioning **20:1
  leverage**.
- **GFCR globally** — GCF anchor investment of **US$125 million** into the Investment Window,
  alongside a **US$125 million** Grant Window target, across 17 countries.
- **Regional Scientific Hub in Aqaba** and a **Gulf of Aqaba Coordination Mechanism for the
  Marine-Coastal Environment among Jordan, Egypt and Saudi Arabia**, plus a **regional coral
  database and monitoring programmes** — all named programme deliverables **[sourced]**.

**[judgement]** That last item is important: the programme intends to build a *regional coral
database and monitoring mechanism*. A platform that already produces standardised,
reproducible, per-event records with fixed IDs is a candidate contribution to it.

### 2.3 GEF — already funding the same reserve

**[sourced]** GEF project **10905**, *"Supporting the Operation and Effective Performance
Management of the Aqaba Marine Reserve, Jordan"*:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| GEF grant | **USD 663,073** |
| Co-financing | **USD 5,500,000** |
| Implementing agency | **IUCN** |
| Executing agency | **UNDP Jordan Country Office** |
| Approved for implementation | **28 April 2023** · GEF-7, Medium-size Project, Biodiversity |
| Objective | *"enhance and strengthen the management effectiveness, equity and operational capabilities of Aqaba Marine Reserve through capacity building and participatory approaches"* |

**[judgement]** A live, funded, IUCN/UNDP-executed project explicitly about **management
effectiveness and operational capability** at the exact reserve. "Operational capability" is
what a monitoring and early-warning platform *is*. This is the second named channel, and it
is already in implementation.

### 2.4 PERSGA — the regional body

**[sourced]** The Regional Organization for the Conservation of the Environment of the Red
Sea and Gulf of Aden **provides technical support and coordinates marine monitoring
programmes across its member countries** and provides the institutional framework for
coordination among member governments.

**[judgement]** PERSGA is the natural route for the multi-country version of the product, and
the natural owner of a shared standard. Slower and more political than UNDP, but it is the
body that already coordinates Red Sea monitoring.

## 3 · Policy instruments that create the obligation

**[judgement]** Funding follows obligation. Five instruments generate duties this platform
helps discharge:

| Instrument | Obligation | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| **GFCR Outcome 1** | "Water quality/land-ocean interface projects" **[sourced]** | Direct programmatic fit |
| **GFCR Outcome 4 — Recover** | Crisis plans, response mechanisms, **parametric reef insurance** **[sourced]** | Parametric insurance needs a *trigger metric*. A validated exposure model is exactly that. See §5. |
| **SDG 13.3** (cited in the concept note) | "impact reduction and **early warning**" **[sourced]** | The programme's own SDG mapping names early warning |
| **30×30 / GBF Target 3** | Demonstrate *effective* management, not just designation | Event-scale monitoring is stronger evidence than an annual survey |
| **UNESCO tentative listing** — Aqaba Marine Reserve (Jordan) and Gulf of Aqaba reefs (Saudi Arabia) **[sourced]** | Nomination dossiers must show monitoring and management capacity | "Strengthens your World Heritage nomination" motivates a government more than "improves sampling efficiency" |

## 4 · The realistic funding sequence

**[judgement]**

| Stage | Source | Amount | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Now → 3 months** | Hackathon, HTU, ABOFA exposure | in-kind | Validated backtest + working demo |
| **3 → 12 months** | **GFCR GoA programme (UNDP Jordan)** or **GEF 10905 (IUCN/UNDP)** | **$50k – 250k** | One real deployment, one flood season, honest metrics |
| **12 → 24 months** | Saudi giga-project pilot — **commercial, not grant** | **$150k – 400k** | Second site, transferability, first real revenue |
| **24 → 36 months** | GFCR Growth phase / PERSGA regional | **$500k – 2M** | Multi-country Gulf of Aqaba deployment |

**[judgement]** Note what is absent: **venture capital**. A dozen institutional buyers and
consultancy-shaped scaling is not a VC-returns profile. Grant-to-contract is the correct path
and a legitimate one.

## 5 · The parametric insurance angle — worth a slide

**[sourced]** GFCR Outcome 4 explicitly includes *"mechanisms in place for rapid financial
support to reef-first SMEs and MPAs impacted by shocks. This includes the use of **parametric
reef insurance**."*

**[judgement]** Parametric insurance pays out automatically when a measurable trigger is
crossed — no loss adjustment. Existing reef parametric products (e.g. in the Caribbean) use
**wind speed** as the trigger, because it is unambiguous and independently measured.

**There is no equivalent trigger for a sediment event.** A validated, reproducible,
independently-auditable exposure model is precisely what a parametric sediment product would
need. That is a genuinely novel product direction sitting inside a funding programme that has
already committed to parametric reef insurance.

**Caveat [judgement]:** this requires a validation record far stronger than a hackathon
backtest — insurers need years of events and demonstrated skill. Present it as a **Phase 3
direction with a named funding programme behind it**, not as a near-term revenue line.

## 6 · The ABOFA opportunity, specifically

**[sourced]** Strong prototypes exhibit at **ABOFA — Aqaba Blue: Ocean Future in Action,
9–13 September 2026**, alongside the **Regional Ocean Summit**, hosted with the Aqaba
Development Corporation.

**[judgement]** This is a **procurement room**, not a demo slot. ASEZA, ADC, UNDP Jordan,
IUCN ROWA, regional environment ministries and Red Sea development representatives are the
plausible attendee list — the same organisations named in §2.

Three consequences:

1. **Build the demo for that room**, not only for the hackathon judges.
2. **Have a one-page institutional brief**: problem, what it does, what it costs, what it
   needs from ASEZA. Not a pitch deck — something a director can hand to a colleague.
3. **The most valuable outcome is one named contact** — realistically at UNDP Jordan or
   ASEZA's Environment Commission — who agrees to a follow-up. Worth more than winning.
