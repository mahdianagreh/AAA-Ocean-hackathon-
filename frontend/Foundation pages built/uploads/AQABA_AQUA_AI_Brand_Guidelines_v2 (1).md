> **Marine Intelligence • Environmental AI • Ocean Technology**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Logo System

## Primary Logo

-   Full-color gradient logo on white background.
-   Preferred version for websites, presentations, and marketing.

## Monochrome Versions

-   Navy (#0A1F4D)
-   White (for dark backgrounds)

## Clear Space

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the inner wave around all
sides.

## Minimum Size

-   Digital: 32 px
-   Print: 12 mm

## Never

-   Stretch the logo
-   Rotate it
-   Add shadows
-   Change colors
-   Place on busy backgrounds
-   Add outlines

135deg,
#0A1F4D 0%,

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Radius

-   Small: 8px

-   Large: 16px
-   Cards: 20px

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Components

## Buttons

Primary: - Background: #0A1F4D - Text: White

Secondary: - White background - Navy border

Accent: - Aqua background - Hover: Marine Teal

## Cards

-   White
-   Radius: 20px
-   Border: #DCE5EC
-   Padding: 24px

## Inputs

-   Height: 48px
-   Radius: 12px
-   Border: #DCE5EC
-   Focus: #00B7C3

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. Icons

Use:

-   Outline icons
-   Rounded corners
-   2px stroke
-   Minimal geometric style

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. Imagery

Preferred:

-   Ocean
-   Coral reefs
-   Satellite imagery
-   Coastlines
-   Scientific research
-   Marine sensors
-   AI dashboards

Avoid:

-   Cartoon fish
-   Boats
-   Anchors
-   Heavy textures

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. Motion

Duration: 200--350ms

Easing:

``` css
ease-in-out
```

Animations should resemble calm water movement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
