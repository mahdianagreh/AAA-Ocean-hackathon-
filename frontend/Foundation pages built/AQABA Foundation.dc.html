A flood in the desert is a marine event. Nothing treats it as one.

Rain over the mountains doesn't soak into the desert — the ground is too hard, too dry. It becomes a fast-moving flood within hours. That flood carries a huge sediment load down the wadis and straight into the sea. The sediment smothers the reef before anyone on the coast knows it's coming.
